A tiny thrift shop with a big imagination

Welcome to the Moth's Cabinet.

Guilded Moth Thrifting is built around the fun part of thrifting: never knowing what you'll discover next. Instead of handing you a generic outfit, each bundle starts with your own style and turns into a little treasure hunt.

Because thrift inventory changes constantly, exact brands and exact pieces aren't guaranteed. Your preferences are the map; the thrift store is the adventure.

Before the hunt

FAQ

What is a thrift bundle?

A curated collection of thrifted clothing chosen around the size, style, colors, fit, and preferences you provide.

Does the bundle size mean my clothing size?

No. The bundle size determines how many pieces you receive. Your clothing size is collected separately during customization.

Can I send a Pinterest board?

Absolutely. A Pinterest board is welcome as visual inspiration. It does not guarantee that exact pieces from the board will be found.

Can I request specific colors or styles?

Yes. You can list favorites, dislikes, specific requests, and exclusions. Flexibility helps because thrift inventory is unpredictable.

Are exact brands guaranteed?

No. The goal is to match your overall vibe and preferences, not guarantee a particular brand or exact item unless explicitly stated.

Do you ship internationally?

No. Guilded Moth Thrifting currently ships within the United States only.

Is shipping included?

No. Shipping is calculated separately based on your US shipping location and shown in your order summary.
